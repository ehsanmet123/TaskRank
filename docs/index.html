TaskRank Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

TaskRank is a task-ranking application operated by the TaskRank developer. This policy explains how TaskRank handles information when you use the app.

Information we handle

TaskRank stores the task titles, completion status, order, and timestamps that you enter. By default, this information is stored locally on your device.

Google cloud sync is optional. If you choose to sign in with Google and use cloud sync, TaskRank uses Google sign-in through Supabase to associate your account with your synced task data. Synced task data includes task titles, completion status, order, and timestamps. Supabase processes and stores this data in order to provide the sync feature.

TaskRank does not sell your personal information. The current version does not show advertisements and does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

How we use and share information

We use your task data only to provide the task list and optional cloud-sync features. Your Google account information is used only to authenticate optional sync. TaskRank shares information with Supabase only when you choose cloud sync. Data is transmitted using encrypted connections.

Retention and deletion

Local task data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Synced data remains associated with your account while you use cloud sync.

To request deletion of your cloud-sync account and associated synced tasks, email support.taskrank@gmail.com with the subject line “Delete my TaskRank account”. We will verify the request and delete the associated synced task data and account information within 30 days.

Children

TaskRank is not directed to children. Do not use Google cloud sync if you are not permitted to use a Google account under the laws that apply to you.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when TaskRank changes. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact support.taskrank@gmail.com.
